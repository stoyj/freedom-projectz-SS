Uganda

For the over 15 years, Freedom Projectz has
faithfully served the people of Uganda,
through God's blessings and provision, to
empower future leaders with education
opportunities, and provide sustainable
solutions to meet the practical needs of the
communities. Tony and JoAnne have seen
God's redemptive and healing power as they
have been witnesses to the transformation of
people through Jesus Christ and His Holy
Spirit. Friends have turned into family, and
Uganda holds a special place in the heart of
this ministry and the Ramos family.

How We Serve

Education | Scholarships

We partner with in-country education programs to
provide scholarships for students from grade
school through college.

Medical Clinics

We provide basic healthcare & medical education
to thousands in an annual medical clinic in Katwe.

Community Support

We support the most urgent needs of local
communities through sustainable programs and
solutions for clean water and nutritious food.
