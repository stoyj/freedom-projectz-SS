Freedom for All

He has set us free to serve one another in love; that is how His freedom grows

Galatians 5:13-14 (MSG)

Freedom Projectz was born out of a desire for people to know the limitless love and freedom that God has for humanity. The Freedom God has for humanity is twofold. It is Freedom from sin, shame, abandonment, and hopelessness and it is Freedom to wholeness, peace, healing, hope and the ability to dream. This is done by partnering with organizations on the ground who are serving their local regions by offering community support for sustainable programs based around education, health care and community advancement.

Here's How We Serve

Education

We train teachers and sponsor students from elementary to college age.

Clean Water

We partner with other non-profis to provide safe, reliable, and sustainble water solutions.

We provide safe and nutritious meals and partner with local farmers to create sustainable food sources.

Healthcare

We provide basic healthcare through on-site medical clinics with doctors and nurses.

Spiritual Support

We perform water baptisms and baptisms of the Holy Spirit.

Teaching the Word of God

We provide teaching and training to strengthen church leaders and local communities to know and understand God's Word to futher the Gospel.
